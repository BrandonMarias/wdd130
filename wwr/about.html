Rafting River Fighter

A family-owned business with over 30 years of experience, dedicated to offering unforgettable, safe, and thrilling adventures led by expert guides on the most exciting rivers.

History

Rafting River Fighter started floating the river in 1995. We have been providing unforgettable experiences for over 30 years. We are a family owned and operated business that is dedicated to providing you with the best experience possible.

We have a team of highly trained, certified guides dedicated to safety, sustainability, and fun. Whether you are an experienced paddler or hitting the whitewater for the first time, our goal is to build memories that will last a lifetime.
